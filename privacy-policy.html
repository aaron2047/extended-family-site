Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Extended Family Integrated Services, LLC

This policy explains what information this website collects and how we handle it. It applies to this website only.

Please do not send private health information through this website or by regular email. Call us at (267) 891-5600 instead.

Information we collect

Information you give us

This website does not have a form that collects information. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number, and message. We use it only to respond to your inquiry.

Information collected automatically

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and like most hosts it may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics, or tracking tools.

Third-party content

Some content, such as the photograph on our home page, is loaded from a third-party service (Unsplash). When your browser loads it, that service may receive your IP address.

The "Contact" section offers links that open Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo Mail. If you click one, you leave this website and that provider's own privacy policy applies.

How we use and share information

  • To respond to your questions and inquiries about services.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • When required by law.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

Email is not secure

Regular email and web forms are not secure ways to send health information. Please share only your name, contact details, and a general description of what you are looking for. Do not include diagnoses, medical records, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive details.

Links to other websites

This website may link to other sites, such as Pennsylvania state agencies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

This website is intended for adults, including family members and guardians. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete an inquiry you sent us, to the extent the law allows, by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new effective date.
