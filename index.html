A few helpful answers.

Who are these services for?
Our focus is on individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and their families. Eligibility and authorization depend on the specific service and funding source.
How are services coordinated?
Extended Family Integrated Services, LLC is enrolled with the Pennsylvania Department of Human Services and works through the Home and Community-Based Services registry. Call to discuss service options and any required coordination with your county program.
How do I learn about pricing and eligibility?
Call (267) 891-5600 to discuss your needs, current service availability, funding, and eligibility. Services are not sold as a fixed package because individual needs and authorizations vary.
