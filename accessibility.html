Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Extended Family Integrated Services, LLC

We serve people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and their families, so we want everyone to be able to use this website. We are working to make it meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we have done

  • A "Skip to content" link for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • Headings and landmarks that assistive technology can read, and links with descriptive text.
  • Text alternatives for informational images.
  • Layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops, and respect reduced-motion settings.
  • A pause control on moving content.

Known limitations

Our brochure and business card artwork are published as images, and some text inside them may be difficult to read with a screen reader. We can provide this information in another format on request.

Request information in another format

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in large print, plain language, audio, or another format, please tell us what you need and we will help.
